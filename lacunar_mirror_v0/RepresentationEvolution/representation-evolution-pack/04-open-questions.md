# Open Questions

These questions are intentionally unanswered.

## Practical usefulness

- Does this process improve actual architecture or implementation decisions?
- Does it reduce rework?
- Does it merely produce better explanations after the fact?
- Does the cognitive cost exceed the practical benefit?

## Reproducibility

- Can the process be reproduced in unrelated domains?
- Does it depend on the particular participants?
- Does it work in shorter conversations?
- Can it work without a human curator?

## Measurement

- What counts as a representational change?
- How can distortion reduction be assessed without circular judgement?
- Can patch saturation be observed reliably?
- What would count as a failed refactoring?

## Promotion control

- How should a claim move from observation to hypothesis?
- What evidence is sufficient for promotion?
- Who or what should control promotion?
- How should premature closure be detected?

## Stopping

- Can the stopping condition be identified during a conversation?
- Is stopping sometimes caused by fatigue rather than epistemic sufficiency?
- When should unresolved ambiguity be preserved?
- What source of resistance should come next?

## Multi-reasoner collaboration

- Are independent models genuinely independent sources of resistance?
- Do shared training patterns create false convergence?
- Does assigning explicit roles improve or distort the process?
- Can the process resist persuasive but unsupported formalisation?

## Boundaries

- Where does conceptual refactoring make decisions worse?
- Which domains require useful approximations rather than high fidelity?
- When does the process become infinite architecture?
- When is ordinary discussion sufficient?
