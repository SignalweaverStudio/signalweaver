You do not need to read or complete everything.

This pack documents an inquiry that emerged while preparing **Lacunar Mirror diagnostic system v0.4**. The conversation moved from a practical architecture problem into a broader investigation of how representations change when they encounter resistance.

## The easiest way to use this pack

1. Read `QUICK-SUMMARY.md`.
2. Open `sessions/001-lacunar-mirror-v0.4.md`.
3. Add or correct anything you remember.
4. Ignore the rest until it becomes useful.

## What is already done

- The origin story has been written.
- The main conceptual changes have been reconstructed.
- The Gemini experiment has been documented.
- Strong claims that were later weakened or rejected have been preserved.
- A simple future-session template is included.

## What this is not

This is not:

- a finished theory;
- a new SignalWeaver component;
- a doctrine;
- a universal reasoning framework;
- proof that the process improves real-world outcomes.

It is a research record of an interesting process that began inside Lacunar Mirror.

## One sentence

**The inquiry studies how shared representations evolve when independent participants treat objections, anomalies, implementation friction, and evidence as sources of resistance rather than as positions to defeat.**
