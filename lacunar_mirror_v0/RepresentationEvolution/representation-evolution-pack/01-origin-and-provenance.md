# Origin and Provenance

## Origin

This inquiry emerged during preparation for **Lacunar Mirror diagnostic system v0.4**.

Lacunar Mirror was being developed as an interaction-dynamics research instrument based on timing telemetry rather than semantic content.

The working pipeline was approximately:

```text
Telemetry
→ Physics
→ Diagnostics
→ Visualisation
→ Session Analysis
```

The immediate design problem was not philosophical. It was architectural.

The project needed to decide what the diagnostic layer should contain and how to prevent interpretation from contaminating measurement.

## Initial pressure

The discussion was constrained by a practical question:

> Are we building the next diagnostic system on the right conceptual foundations?

The initial architecture used terms such as:

- telemetry;
- physics;
- diagnostics;
- sessions;
- transitions;
- visualisation;
- interpretation.

As the discussion progressed, several of those terms appeared to be carrying multiple responsibilities.

## Participants

### Nic Dredge

Originator and curator of the inquiry. Nic maintained continuity, selected which exchanges to continue, and repeatedly prevented the conversation from becoming a self-contained doctrine.

### ChatGPT

Participated in the initial Lacunar Mirror inquiry and later reviewed the Gemini transfer experiment. It frequently challenged premature promotions and looked for overloaded conceptual boundaries.

### Gemini

Received a distilled version of the original conversation. Gemini extended, formalised, criticised, and sometimes over-promoted the emerging ideas. Its responses became additional sources of resistance.

## Historical sequence

1. Lacunar Mirror v0.4 diagnostic architecture was about to be designed.
2. Diagnostics were questioned as a potentially overloaded layer.
3. Observables, structures, diagnostics, and interpretation were separated.
4. The conversation widened into methodology, constraints, representations, and error locality.
5. Concepts were reframed as compressions.
6. The stopping condition became: stop when another abstraction would no longer reduce distortion.
7. A condensed account was passed to Gemini.
8. Gemini introduced utility thresholds, bounded rationality, admissibility, temporal binding, cost placement, and alignment.
9. ChatGPT repeatedly weakened claims that exceeded the evidence.
10. The interaction itself became the object of interest.

## Provenance rule

Any future description of the inquiry should preserve this origin:

> It began as a practical architectural discussion about Lacunar Mirror diagnostic system v0.4.

It should not be retroactively described as having begun as a universal theory of reasoning, governance, or admissibility.
