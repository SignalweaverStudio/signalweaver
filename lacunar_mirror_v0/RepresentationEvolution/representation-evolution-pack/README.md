# Representation Evolution Inquiry

## Status

**Early research record — provisional and unvalidated**

This collection preserves an inquiry that emerged during the architectural design of **Lacunar Mirror diagnostic system v0.4**.

The original discussion concerned the separation of telemetry, physics, observables, structures, diagnostics, visualisation, and interpretation. During that work, the participants repeatedly revised the conceptual representation itself. A later transfer experiment with Gemini exposed further patterns, failure modes, and stopping conditions.

The collection records:

- the practical origin of the inquiry;
- the sequence of representational changes;
- the objections that triggered those changes;
- claims that were accepted, weakened, or rejected;
- the emerging observation of resistance-based collaboration across independent reasoners.

## Research posture

The material should be read as:

- observations;
- interpretations;
- heuristics;
- working hypotheses;
- rejected or weakened claims;
- open questions.

It should not be read as:

- a theorem;
- a universal architecture;
- an operationally validated diagnostic;
- a product specification;
- an extension of SignalWeaver.

## Relationship to Lacunar Mirror

The inquiry belongs historically to Lacunar Mirror because it emerged from the attempt to design its v0.4 diagnostic architecture.

It is documented separately because the process appears potentially relevant beyond that project.

## Relationship to SignalWeaver

SignalWeaver was later used as a comparison point. Similarities involving governance, admissibility, transitions, and explicit boundaries were observed.

This documentation does **not** place the inquiry inside SignalWeaver.
