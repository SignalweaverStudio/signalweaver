# Key Phrases

These phrases are preserved because they marked important changes in the inquiry.

- Observation precedes interpretation.
- Every transformation exposes its assumptions.
- Structures are described before they are explained.
- Methodology is itself open to validation.
- Components are replaceable; epistemic independence is not.
- A session is a partition of time. A transition is an event.
- Only anomalies propagate upward.
- Architecture should reduce the cost of discovering error.
- Design systems such that surprise has somewhere legitimate to go.
- An abstraction is a compression.
- What have we compressed that reality keeps trying to pull apart?
- Seek the least distorted abstraction.
- Reality exposes the abstraction.
- The other participant is another source of resistance.
- What responsibility is attached to the wrong conceptual object?
- What representation would allow reality to disagree with us more precisely?
- The next abstraction should not be added unless it reduces distortion.
- Perfect fidelity is a luxury; useful approximations are a necessity.
- Refactoring is event-driven rather than continuous.
- Patch saturation is structural debt. Prediction error is external mismatch.
- Where was the cost of admissibility paid?
- Who paid it?
- Responsibility without authority creates a liability sink.
- Misalignment among benefit, authority, visibility, and liability is a strong indicator of governance risk.
- Has this promotion been earned?
- The next move belongs to a new source of resistance.
