# Status Labels

Use these labels to stop ideas from becoming more certain in memory than they were in the conversation.

## Observation

Something directly noticed in the conversation, implementation, or evidence.

## Interpretation

A proposed meaning of an observation.

## Heuristic

A practical guide that may be useful without being universally true.

## Working hypothesis

A claim that should be exposed to disconfirming evidence.

## Promotion candidate

An idea that may deserve stronger status if further evidence supports it.

## Rejected claim

A formulation found to be false, misleading, or too strong.

## Weakened claim

A useful idea retained in a more modest form.

## Open question

A question for which the conversation did not earn an answer.

## Evidence needed

The implementation, observation, case, or experiment required before promotion.
