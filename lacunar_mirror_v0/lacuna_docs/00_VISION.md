# LACUNA VISION

## Purpose

Lacuna is an instrument for revealing the invisible dynamics of human-computer interaction.

It is not a productivity application.

It is not a wellbeing monitor.

It is not an attention tracker.

It does not attempt to infer thoughts, emotions, motives, intelligence, creativity, focus, or cognitive state.

Lacuna observes physical interaction timing, integrates those observations into a continuous internal field, and expresses the evolution of that field through a calm, physically believable visual material.

Its role is not to explain the user.

Its role is to reveal what was already there.

## Core Architecture

Lacuna is composed of four distinct layers:

1. Observation layer  
   Captures privacy-preserving interaction timing such as mouse movement cadence, keyboard cadence, tempo, irregularity, and idle duration.

2. Integration engine  
   Converts observations into a continuous physical state.

3. Lacuna field  
   The internal state variables through which the instrument evolves.

4. Visual instrument  
   Expresses the field through a coherent material response.

The renderer does not respond directly to keystrokes or mouse events.

It responds to the Lacuna field.

## Current Form

The first implementation is a desktop instrument.

The present dot renderer is a development and validation tool, not the finished interface.

The intended desktop product is a quiet optical overlay: a movable window into the Liquid Lens.

## Future Forms

Possible future embodiments include:

- Desktop Overlay
- Desktop Instrument
- Studio Edition
- Wearable Lens
- Smart Glasses

The implementation may change.

The identity must remain recognisably Lacuna.

## North Star

Lacuna should feel like an impossible scientific instrument that reveals an invisible physical process.

Not a dashboard.

Not a productivity widget.

Not an assistant.

An instrument.
