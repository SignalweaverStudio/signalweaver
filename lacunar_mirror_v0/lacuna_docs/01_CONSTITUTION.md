# LACUNA CONSTITUTION

Version 0.1  
Status: Draft

## Article I — The Instrument

Lacuna is an instrument.

Never a dashboard.

Never a gamified interface.

Never an assistant attempting to direct behaviour.

Its role is observation rather than instruction.

## Article II — The Field

The visualisation reveals the state of the Lacuna field.

It does not visualise raw input events directly.

Mouse movement, keyboard timing, and future acquisition sources drive the integration engine.

The renderer responds only to the resulting field state.

## Article III — Truthfulness

Lacuna never claims to know what the user is thinking.

It never infers:

- mood
- attention
- creativity
- motivation
- intelligence
- emotion
- cognitive load
- productivity

Every visual change must be attributable to measurable state variables produced by the engine.

## Article IV — Physical Believability

Every visible behaviour should appear to arise from a coherent physical material.

The renderer may simplify reality.

It may stylise reality.

It must not abandon physical plausibility.

Motion should appear to obey stable internal rules.

## Article V — Calmness

The instrument remains visually calm in every operational state.

Intensity may increase.

Energy may increase.

Complexity may increase.

Aggression must never emerge.

Lacuna should remain comfortable to inhabit for many hours.

## Article VI — Material Cohesion

The visual medium is continuous.

It may:

- stretch
- fold
- flow
- separate
- recombine
- form standing waves
- form smooth ribbons
- form rounded droplets
- distort optically

It must not form:

- spikes
- cellular textures
- clustered holes
- sea-urchin structures
- trypophobia-triggering patterns
- chaotic spectacle

Ferrofluid is an inspiration for material qualities, not a literal behavioural model.

## Article VII — Peripheral Presence

Lacuna should occupy peripheral vision comfortably.

It may invite attention.

It must never demand attention.

The user should not feel obliged to monitor it.

## Article VIII — Design Integrity

Every future embodiment must remain recognisably the same instrument.

Desktop.

Studio.

Wearable.

Lens.

Smart glasses.

The delivery mechanism may change.

The design language must remain continuous.

## Article IX — Research Integrity

Observation precedes interpretation.

The acquisition layer records interaction timing.

The engine integrates those observations.

The renderer expresses the resulting field.

Human interpretation occurs afterwards.

Lacuna itself performs no psychological interpretation.

## Article X — User Testimony

The user may add context labels or notes.

These are self-authored testimony.

Lacuna may store and display them.

Lacuna must not silently reinterpret, score, diagnose, or classify their meaning.

## Article XI — The Liquid Lens

Liquid Lens is the primary visual technology through which Lacuna expresses its field.

It is inspired by:

- precision optics
- liquid lenses
- ferrofluid
- optical coatings
- scientific metrology
- suspended media
- sapphire
- glass
- dark fluids
- thin-film interference

Liquid Lens borrows qualities such as:

- cohesion
- density
- depth
- smooth flow
- suspended structure
- optical refraction
- restrained iridescence

It does not simulate literal ferrofluid spikes or magnetic cellular patterns.

## Article XII — Continuity of Meaning

The visual instrument expresses state, not value.

It does not display:

- good
- bad
- success
- failure
- focus
- distraction
- virtue
- weakness

It reveals dynamics without judgement.

## Final Statement

Lacuna does not attempt to understand the user.

It makes visible a physical field generated from interaction dynamics and presents that field through a calm, truthful, and physically believable instrument.

Its purpose is not to explain the user.

Its purpose is to reveal what was already there.
