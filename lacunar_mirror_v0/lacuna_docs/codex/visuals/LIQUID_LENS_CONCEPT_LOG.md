# LIQUID LENS CONCEPT LOG

## Selected Direction

Concave Well / Liquid Lens Instrument

## Core Materials

- concave sapphire crystal
- subtle purple and green AR edge coating
- raw naval brass tension ring
- microscopic fasteners
- dark gunmetal stainless steel
- tungsten core
- index-matched reactive fluid
- dark optical cavity

## Rejected Visual Behaviours

- cellular tension
- clustered holes
- ferrofluid spikes
- sea-urchin structures
- trypophobia-triggering textures
- chaotic fragmentation

## Preferred Behaviours

- smooth standing waves
- broad concentric ripples
- flowing ribbons
- directional folds
- rounded droplets
- slow orbital separation
- gradual recombination
- optical depth
- calm return to equilibrium

## Key Design Insight

Ferrofluid is the inspiration, not the literal behaviour.

Lacuna visualises the internal field, not raw user actions.
