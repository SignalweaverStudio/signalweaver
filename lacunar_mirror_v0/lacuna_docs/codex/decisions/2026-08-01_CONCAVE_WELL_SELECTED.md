# DESIGN DECISION — CONCAVE WELL

Date: 2026-08-01  
Status: Selected leading direction

## Decision

Adopt the Concave Well as the leading physical identity for Lacuna.

## Reasons

- balances scientific credibility with emotional warmth
- produces depth through real optical form
- supports a calm Liquid Lens material
- avoids consumer-electronics styling
- remains believable as a precision instrument
- can inform desktop and future wearable embodiments

## Constraints

- brass must remain restrained and instrument-like
- no steampunk styling
- no visible UI
- no glowing lights
- no spikes
- no cellular structures
- no trypophobia triggers
- fluid remains smooth and cohesive

## Implication for Desktop Overlay

The overlay should act as a window into the Liquid Lens rather than reproduce the whole physical housing.
